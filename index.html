SCORING GUIDE

Score each decision factor.

For each decision factor, consider your assigned U.S. state and choose likelihood and impact from 1 to 5. The risk score, priority ranking, and heat map update automatically.

Likelihood

  1. Very low — Rare or unlikely
  2. Low — Possible but not expected
  3. Moderate — Reasonably possible
  4. High — Likely
  5. Very high — Very likely

Impact

  1. Minimal — Small effect on success
  2. Minor — Manageable effect
  3. Moderate — Meaningful effect
  4. Major — Serious effect
  5. Critical — Could prevent success
Risk score = Likelihood × ImpactThe heat map counts scored factors. Priority 1 is the highest score; ties share a rank.

Priority rank is one plus the number of factors with a higher score. For example, two factors tied at rank 1 are followed by rank 3.
